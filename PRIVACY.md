# Privacy Notice

Hair by Thuli collects only the information needed to manage appointments and salon communication:

- name
- email address
- phone number
- typed location supplied by the client
- appointment and service details
- optional comments

Appointment data is visible to authorized salon staff for scheduling and communication. Comments are reviewed before public display. Location is optional and is never requested automatically.

Clients should be given a way to request correction or deletion of their data before launch. Configure database backups with encryption and define a retention period for completed and cancelled bookings. Do not collect payment details in this application without a compliant payment provider.

This notice should be reviewed and adapted to the salon's local legal requirements before production launch.
